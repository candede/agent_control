Provides authenticated, permission-controlled access to saved organizational evidence about Microsoft 365 Copilot and Copilot agents.

Helps end users discover relevant agents, understand their documented capabilities and availability, explore shared learning, and identify practical first tasks. Discovering an agent does not grant permission to use it.

Supports authorized staff with adoption and usage analysis, licensing and service readiness, agent inventory, reporting snapshots, adoption briefs, learning cards, follow-up tracking, and permitted synchronization and management operations.

Use Agent Control for evidence-based organizational answers and recommendations, preserving reporting periods and data limitations. Available tools, data, and actions depend on the signed-in user's permissions and server privacy settings. Combine its evidence with other connected tools when needed to complete user-requested workflows.
